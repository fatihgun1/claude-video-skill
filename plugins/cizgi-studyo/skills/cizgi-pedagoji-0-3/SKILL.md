# Ages 0-3 profile

Use `"profile": "pedagoji-0-3"` in `bolum.json`. The profile already sets:
- slower speech (tempo 0.85)
- longer pauses (lead 0.6 s, gap 0.5 s)
- a 3 s default hold so the child can imitate
- shots of at least 4 s

It also warns about long sentences, counting above three, more than two characters and camera moves other than static, push in and pull out.

Read **[kurallar.md](kurallar.md)** before writing the script. Summary:

- **Structure**: 50–90 s, 5–8 shots. The first shot is a greeting (`selam`) and the last a goodbye.
- **One body movement per shot**, in this order:
  1. say what happens
  2. the character shows it
  3. invite the child ("Sen de …!")
  4. hold (`hold` 3 s with movement, 1 s without)
  5. a short praise
- **Language**: sentences of 2–6 words, concrete words, onomatopoeia and repetition. No irony, fear, villains or fights. Count to three at most, written as words.
- **Movements by age**: wave and clap for everyone; pointing from 12 months; walking from 12–15 months; running, jumping and feet from 18–24 months. Use only actions the library has. A missing one goes to `new_assets`.
- **At most two characters**, doing the same movement (imitation model), and only one speaking at a time.
- **Picture and sound**: pastel colours, steady camera, soft transitions. No flashes, fast cuts or sudden loud sounds.

## At each gate, check with the person
- Are numbers, colour names and body parts correct?
- Does every shot have one movement and a hold?
- Is there anything scary, sudden or too fast?

## Delivery note for the video description
Screen time is not recommended under 18–24 months. Watch together with a parent, for a short time.
