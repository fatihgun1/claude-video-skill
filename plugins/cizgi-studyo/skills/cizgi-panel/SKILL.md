# Editor panel

In a Claude session, open it with the `panel_ac` tool of the plugin's `cizgi-studyo` MCP server (see the `cizgi-studyo` skill): that panel lives as long as the session. The CLI below starts one that lives until it is stopped.

```bash
CLI="node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/engine/src/cli.mjs"
$CLI panel <klasör>                  # every bolum.json under the folder; http://127.0.0.1:4810/
$CLI panel <bolum.json>              # that episode's folder, with that episode open
$CLI panel <klasör> --port 4900
```

## Steps
1. Open the panel at once, before anything else, even when the request already describes a video: `panel_ac` (or the CLI below when the MCP server is missing), then open the URL in the built-in browser.
2. If the request is a new video or story, follow the **cizgi-proje** steps from step 2: the episode is made with the person, question by question, and every step shows in the panel. If it names an existing episode, open it (`panel_ac` with `bolum`) and ask what they want to change.
3. While the panel is open, show every change there: after you edit `bolum.json` or `hikaye.json`, tell the person what to look at (a scene, a shot, **İncele**).

Open the project root (the folder holding `ornekler/` and `bolumler/`) when the person works on several episodes: the panel then lists them all and switches between them without a restart. Run it in the background (it keeps serving until stopped) and give the person the printed URL. In the Claude desktop app, open it in the built-in browser. The layout needs a window at least 1180 px wide; narrower windows stack the panes.

## What the person can do there
- **Narrow window** (below 1180px): the rail, the stage and the timeline stay on screen; the left panel and the properties open over the stage as drawers, one at a time (the properties from **Özellikler** in the top bar). ✕ or Esc closes a drawer. The left panel starts closed there; the wide layout keeps its own choice.
- **Left rail:** icons for Sahneler, Karakter, Hareket, Mekân, Nesne, Ses, Müzik and Kamera. A click opens that panel in the one left pane; a click on the open icon (or ✕) closes the pane and the stage grows. The choice is remembered in the browser.
  - **Sahneler** lists the open episode's scenes (background thumbnail, name, shot count, length, error count) and their shots.
    - A shot row selects the shot.
    - Drag a shot row onto another shot or onto a scene's header to move it there; Alt+↑/↓ does the same from the keyboard and crosses into the next scene at the edge. A scene keeps at least one shot. A shot that moves to another scene plays in that scene's place (background).
    - The selected scene has **Adlandır** (a working name, `scene.title`, not in the film; double-click the name too), **↑ / ↓** (scene order) and **Sil** (the scene with its shots; an episode keeps one scene). **+ Sahne** adds a scene after the selected one: same place, the people of its last shot standing, no lines.
- **Hikâye** (top bar): the story cards of the open episode, saved to `hikaye.json` next to it (a draft, not a gate; it never changes `bolum.json`). The idea and the audience; characters (a library drawing, or "çizilecek" for one still to be drawn); one card per scene with what happens, who is in it (ticks), the feeling and about how long. Cards move with ← → or by dragging their number. A card that became a scene links to it. **Kaydet** writes the file (refused when invalid, or when the file changed on disk meanwhile). **Claude kartları önersin** and **Senaryoya dönüştür** send requests to the Claude session (after saving). When Claude changes `hikaye.json` the open window reloads it, or asks first when there are unsaved edits; closing with unsaved edits asks too.
- **İncele** (on the selected scene in the project tree): a review of that scene.
  - **Kurallar**: checks on the unsaved draft: the validator's messages about the scene, a character not fully in frame (a character standing back counts narrower), two characters at about the same depth drawn over each other, a line too long for two caption lines in the current format, lines not voiced yet, recordings that failed the listening check. A shot id opens that shot.
  - **Kareler**: two stills per shot of the scene from the saved file (`cizgi storyboard <bolum.json> --sahne <id>`, into `inceleme/<scene>/<16x9|9x16>/`). Not the storyboard to approve: no gate needed, unvoiced lines play silent.
  - **Claude'a incelet**: queues a request (as in Claude'a yaz) asking Claude to look at every still and report per shot, with the rule findings attached; Claude suggests fixes and asks before changing anything.
- **Claude'a yaz** (a drawer at the top of the properties; closed it is one line, it opens by itself when Claude asks something): pick the target (the selected character, prop, effect or line; its shot; its scene; the whole episode), write a request, send (Ctrl+Enter). It goes to the Claude session that opened the panel (see the `cizgi-studyo` skill); its state (sırada, Claude'a iletildi, Claude üzerinde çalışıyor, yapıldı, Claude soruyor, olmadı) and Claude's answer show below it. A panel started from the command line has no session and says so. When bolum.json changes on disk, the panel reloads it; with unsaved edits it asks first.
- **Timeline: Sahne | Bölüm**. Sahne shows only the selected shot's scene (the ruler still reads film seconds); Bölüm shows the whole episode.
- Script approval: moving or reordering shots or scenes changes the shot order, so the senaryo approval goes stale after saving (as with adding or deleting shots). A scene name does not.
- **Projeler** (the home page): the panel opens on it unless the address names an episode (`#b=…`) or the panel was started for one. A card per `bolum.json` in the folder with its background, title, shot count, length and approval state, and a search (`/`). A click opens it; with unsaved changes the panel asks first. In the editor, **← Projeler** in the top bar goes back to it (the open episode stays loaded; **Açık bölüme dön** or Esc returns). **Yeni bölüm** asks for a title and an id and writes `bolumler/<id>/bolum.json` from a minimal valid template (one scene, one shot, Ela standing in the park); it never overwrites. The open episode stays in the address (`#b=…`), so a reload keeps it.
- **Timeline** (bottom): a ruler with scene marks and these tracks. Zoom with Ctrl + wheel (around the pointer), **−** / **+**, or **Sığdır** (the scene or episode on show fits the width); the ruler's labels space out as it zooms out. **Mıknatıs** (on by default, remembered in the browser): a dragged edge — an effect, an action's start, a line, the hold, a dropped card — sticks to the playhead, a line's start or end and the shot's start when it comes close, and a dashed guide shows it; switch it off to place things freely. The ruler and the Shot track stay in place while the tracks scroll; near the left or right edge a drag scrolls the timeline. Drag its top edge (or ↑/↓ on it) to make it taller or shorter. The side panels have the same dividers at the stage's left and right edges (←/→). A double-click on a divider puts that size back; the browser remembers the sizes, and each side panel stays within 40% of the window.
  - **Shot:** one block per shot, as wide as it lasts, with its background as the picture and a red badge when it has errors. Click to select; drag the block to reorder it within its scene (it goes where the pointer is let go; a yellow line shows the place); drag its right edge to change `hold` (it stops where the lines end). **+ Shot** adds a shot after the selected one with the same people standing; **Çoğalt** (Ctrl+D) copies it without its lines; **Sil** (Delete) removes it (a scene keeps at least one shot).
  - **One track per character:** their actions in each shot as clips (a one-off action shorter than its clip holds its last pose; the dark bar is the action itself). Click a clip to select it; drag its left edge to change when it starts. Dragging the first clip's left edge puts a standing action (bekle) before it: the action starts later (not for a walk, which starts with the shot). If that is where a walk stops, the walk stops there too at the same speed (`walk.time` and `walk.dx` follow), so the feet do not slide. In the Hareket panel, a card click replaces the selected action; **+ Ekle** starts that action at the playhead (stored as `cast[].actions: [{action, at}]` after the member's own `action`). Delete removes the selected action.
  - **Replik:** the lines where they are spoken, coloured by speaker. The words are read-only; drag a line later to add a pause before it (`utterance.delay`). A line that failed its listening check and was not accepted has a red dashed outline. Select one to set its loudness (`utterance.volume`), listen to it, and read its listening check.
  - **Altyazı:** each line's caption under it (its text is the line itself). A click turns that caption off or on (`utterance.altyazi: false`); a line's details have the same switch.
  - **Efekt:** the sound effects; drag one sideways to change its `at` (within its shot). The dark bar is the sound's real length.
  - **Müzik:** the background music as its loudness curve (fades, and the dips under the lines). Click it, **Mikser** or **Bölüm ayarları** for track levels (`mix.lines`, `mix.sfx`) and the music (`music`: track, level, fade in/out, level while speaking, loop). Music is chosen in the Müzik panel (**Kullan**) from `library/muzik`.
  - **M / S** next to Replik, Efekt and Müzik mute a track or play it alone in the preview (listening only, not saved).
  - Click or drag the ruler to move the playhead.
- **2B | 3B** (next to 16:9 | 9:16): the preview drawn in 3D from the same episode (watch only: dragging people on the stage is 2D's; an episode with no 3D recipe for a character or place says which). The 3D look (`stil3d`) is chosen in **Bölüm ayarları** → 3B görünüm, and **Taslak 3B video** in Üretim renders a 3D draft (`cizgi render --taslak --3b`).
- **Stage** (middle): the compiled preview, always whole (it fits the room left, never cropped), with sound: lines and effects play in step with the picture (🔊 mutes). Space plays/pauses; ←/→ step one frame, Shift+←/→ one second.
  - When paused inside the selected shot, every cast member and prop has a frame. Click selects it. Drag sideways to move it: it lands on the nearest free mark (sol / orta / sağ) when close, otherwise off it by `dx` (stored as `cast[].dx` / `props[].dx`). Drag a character up to put it further back (higher and smaller, behind the others; `cast[].back` 0..1), down to bring it to the front; with the frame focused, ←/→ and ↑/↓ do the same in small steps. ⇆ turns the selected character around. A walking character shows its path as an arrow; drag the free end to change `walk.dx`.
  - **16:9 / 9:16** compiles the preview for that frame (the episode does not change). **Güvenli alan** shows the action and title safe areas and the caption zone. **Katmanlar** hides background, characters, props or captions in the preview only.
- **Undo** ↶ / **redo** ↷ (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z) step through every change since the episode was opened.
- **Library** (the other rail panels), one per kind. A click applies to the selection; a card can also be **dragged** onto the timeline or the stage, and then it applies where it lands (the places it can land light up, a line on the timeline shows the moment):
  - an effect: at the moment it is let go (on the stage: at the playhead);
  - an action: onto a character's track, starting where it is let go (at the very start of a shot it replaces the first action), or onto a character on the stage (from the playhead);
  - a character onto the stage: it stands where it is let go (the nearest free mark, or off it); onto a shot: added to it;
  - a prop, a place (the whole scene) or a camera card: onto a shot block or the stage;
  - music: anywhere on the timeline.
  What a click does:
  - **Karakter:** cards with the drawing; the views of one person sit together. With a cast member selected, a click swaps them; with nothing selected, it adds the character to the shot.
  - **Hareket:** only the actions drawn for the selected (or the only) cast member's view; hovering plays the action with that character. Actions that break that character's joint limits are dimmed and say why.
  - **Mekân:** background pictures; a click changes the scene's background (every shot of that scene).
  - **Nesne:** a click swaps the selected prop or adds a new one. **+ Dekor** stands it in the scene for all its shots, behind the characters (`scene.dekor`); its frame on the stage drags sideways for its place and up/down for its depth (←/→, ↑/↓ with the frame focused), and the inspector's **Dekor** section has its place, depth, size, mirror and delete. Props marked "dekor" cannot be held.
  - **Ses:** ▶ plays the effect; **+ Ekle** adds it to the shot at the playhead. Effects come only from the library (the plugin's and the project's `kutuphane/sfx`); for a missing one, **Claude'dan yeni efekt iste** sends a request: Claude suggests a CC0 source and downloads it only after you say yes. Project effects left out (no source written in `kutuphane/sfx/KAYNAKLAR.md`, a name the plugin uses) are listed there.
  - **Müzik:** ▶ plays a piece; **Kullan** makes it the episode's background music (its level is in **Mikser**).
  - **Kamera:** framing diagrams and move diagrams; a click sets the shot's camera.
- **Properties** (right) show what is selected, its settings first.
  - **Nothing selected:** the shot: camera, hold, then one row per cast member, prop, sound effect and line (a click opens it), the new-line form and notes. Under them a folded **Sahne** section (its open/closed state is remembered in the browser) holds what applies to every shot of the scene: **Mekân katmanları** (the background's layers, back to front, with their depth: show/hide, move x/y, scale, tint; saved as `scene.katmanlar`; a one-file background has one layer, `tum`), **Işık** (time of day and strength, `scene.isik`), **Altyazının yeri** (alt, üst, orta, and size, `scene.altyazi`) and **Dekor**.
  - **A cast member, prop, effect, line or decor selected** (on the stage, the timeline or its row): only that one, with its details (mark, facing, walk, time, volume…) and its problems; **← <shot>** goes back to the shot.
  - **Bölüm ayarları** (top bar button, the timeline's **Mikser**, or a click on the music clip): track levels (`mix.lines`, `mix.sfx`), the music (`music`) and the episode's captions: none at all, or each edged with its speaker's colour, with a colour per character (`altyazi.kapali`, `altyazi.konusan_rengi`, `characters[].renk`).
- Every change is validated and recompiled into the preview. Errors block saving; messages that name a shot are links to it.
- **Kaydet** (or Ctrl+S) writes `bolum.json`. **Hepsini at** drops all unsaved changes (itself undoable).
- **Production** (the step chips in the top bar: Senaryo, Ses n/m, Storyboard, Render; click one): the state of each step and what to ask for. **Sesleri üret** (step 2, when lines have no audio and the script is approved) runs `cizgi ses` in the background with a progress bar (speaking, listening check, mouth shapes, line by line); when it ends the preview plays the new audio. Without the approval the button is **Claude'dan iste**: it leaves a request for the Claude session, which shows the lines and asks for the approval first. The speech tools are set in `~/.cizgi-studyo/config.json` (`python_tts`, `python_align`, `rhubarb`). On the saved file, the person can start **Taslak video çıkar** at any time (`cizgi render --taslak`: a preview that needs no approval; lines not spoken yet play silent), and **Storyboard karelerini al** when the script is approved and every line has audio (`cizgi storyboard`); both run in the background and their contact sheets and videos show there. The Render chip turns green only for a final render; drafts show as **Taslak n**. Engine messages the panel knows (approval and job errors, project library problems) are shown in Turkish. Approvals are never given in the panel.
- **Problems:** a shot with warnings has a yellow count on the timeline, with errors a red one. Its messages are listed at the top of the properties, and the row they are about (cast member, prop, effect, line) has a "!"; clicking a message opens that row.
- **Colours:** a cast member's details have a colour picker for every colour its rig lets an episode change (`characters[].palette`); they apply in every shot.

Lines can be edited, added and deleted in the panel (a line's details and the form under the lines). When the script is approved, the person must first tick that they know the approval will drop: the script then has to be approved again (**cizgi-senaryo**) and the changed lines spoken again (**cizgi-ses**). Until then the preview plays a new line silent with a guessed length and a changed line with its old recording; a render always needs the real audio. The script approval also covers the shot list too, so adding, copying, deleting or reordering shots makes it stale after saving; the person approves the script again through **cizgi-senaryo**.

## Rules for you
- The panel never approves anything. A saved change makes the storyboard approval stale (the top bar shows it); the person approves again through **cizgi-storyboard**.
- You may edit `bolum.json` and `hikaye.json` while the panel is open: it reloads them, and asks the person first when they have unsaved edits there. Tell the person what you changed, so they do not lose edits of their own.
- New actions, rigs, backgrounds, props or sounds go into the library (the panel reloads it on every request). The panel only chooses among what exists.
- The preview is the composition, not a render. Before showing anyone a video of the result, render a draft and check it with `cizgi qa` (**cizgi-render**).
- The server listens on 127.0.0.1 only. Stop it when the person is done.
